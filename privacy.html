Privacy policy

Bnei Noah Quiz app (Android and web version) · Updated 5 October 2026 · Français

In short: Bnei Noah Quiz does not collect, use, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Who publishes the app

The app is published on Google Play by the developer account associated with the address les.benei.noah@gmail.com. For any question about this policy or your data, write to that address.

Data collected

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address or location, and does not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Data stored on your device

To show your best scores, the app saves your best score for each quiz theme and each game, your current streak in Mystery word, and your settings (language and theme). This information:

  • stays only in the memory of your phone (or of your browser for the web version);
  • is never sent to anyone, including the developer;
  • cannot be used to identify you.

Once installed on the home screen, the web version also keeps a copy of the app’s files (pages, fonts, images) on the device so that it works offline. This copy contains no information about you.

Internet connection and third-party services

The app works entirely offline: the questions and the fonts are included in the app. It contains no third-party services (advertising, analytics, crash reports, social networks). Only the link to this page opens your browser.

The web version and this page are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may record visitors’ IP addresses for security reasons; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, none can be exposed by the app. Scores are stored in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access.

Retention and deletion

Scores and settings are kept as long as the app is installed. To erase them:

  • uninstall the app;
  • or, on Android, open Settings › Apps › Bnei Noah Quiz › Storage › Clear data;
  • for the web version, clear this site’s data in your browser.

Children

The app collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
